# Gattyworks Character IP & Expressions

This document defines the 5 elemental characters for Gattyworks Illustrations.

*(Note: Character designs are currently placeholders and will be finalized later.)*

## 1. Terra (Rock)
- **Visuals**: Sturdy, blocky, solid forms. Earthy tones.
- **Personality**: Grounded, reliable, unshakeable.
- **Metaphors**: Foundation, infrastructure, databases, immutable state, legacy code, solid principles.
- **Roles**: Usually the anchor of an illustration, providing support.

## 2. Spark (Lightning)
- **Visuals**: Jagged lines, glowing energy, dynamic poses. Bright yellow/electric blue.
- **Personality**: Fast, energetic, impatient, bright.
- **Metaphors**: APIs, speed, instant execution, events, signals, hotfixes.
- **Roles**: Moving quickly between other elements, bringing things to life.

## 3. Aqua (Water)
- **Visuals**: Fluid, rounded, droplet-like or wave-like forms. Cool blues/cyans.
- **Personality**: Adaptable, calm, continuous, flowing.
- **Metaphors**: CI/CD pipelines, streams, flow state, data flow, adaptability, user journeys.
- **Roles**: Connecting different parts of a system smoothly.

## 4. Ignis (Fire)
- **Visuals**: Flickering, intense, wispy or sharp flames. Warm reds/oranges.
- **Personality**: Passionate, intense, destructive yet transformative.
- **Metaphors**: Deployment, execution, burnout, deleting/cleaning, high CPU usage, "putting out fires".
- **Roles**: Action-oriented, often showing high-energy states.

## 5. Aero (Wind)
- **Visuals**: Light, breezy, cloud-like or swirling forms. Whites/pale grays/mint.
- **Personality**: Lighthearted, everywhere at once, unseen but felt.
- **Metaphors**: The Cloud, networks, Wi-Fi, abstract concepts, serverless architecture.
- **Roles**: Overseeing things from above, carrying messages.

## Shared Expressions
All characters share standard expressions that can be adapted to their elemental form:
- **Excited**: Eyes wide, glowing/vibrating.
- **Confused**: Question mark floating, form slightly destabilized.
- **Eureka**: Bright flash or lightbulb moment, form stands tall.
- **Frustrated**: Form becoming chaotic (e.g., Fire flaring up, Water boiling).
- **Sleepy**: Form drooping, low energy.
