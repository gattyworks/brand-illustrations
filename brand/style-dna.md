# Brand Style DNA

This document outlines the visual style constraints for generating illustrations for the brand.

## 1. Overall Vibe
- Clean, modern, brand-aligned illustrations.
- Tech-savvy but approachable.
- Subtle humor and clear visual metaphors.

## 2. Line Art & Colors
- **Lines**: Bold, clean vector-style lines.
- **Colors**: Ensure the primary brand color palette is used (define hex codes here). Character-specific colors (defined in their markdown files) should be integrated naturally without clashing with the overall brand.
- **Backgrounds**: Generally keep backgrounds clean or abstract so the characters and the concept stand out.

## 3. Brand Elements
- Illustrations should feel cohesive and instantly recognizable as your brand's assets.
- Keep the style suitable for professional but fun social media channels.

## 4. Text in Images
- If using text in the illustration (like labels or speech bubbles), keep it to 1-3 words max to ensure the AI image generator spells it correctly.
- Alternatively, generate the image without text and add text later in post-production.
