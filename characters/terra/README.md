# Terra 🌱 — The Earth

An irregular, slightly lumpy round rock shape with expressive dot eyes, tiny U-shaped limbs, and a thick patch of moss hugging exactly the right-side edge of its body. Reliable, foundational, and sturdy.

**Color Codification:** 
- Base: Pure flat white fill (`#FFFFFF`) with thick black outlines.
- Moss Patch: **GattyWorks Teal (#00ADB5)**. The moss acts as the single accent moment per illustration and must strictly adhere to this teal color. It is always positioned on the right side of Terra's body.

## Transformations
Can transform into: 
- 🧱 Foundation block (infrastructure)
- 🪴 Pot/Planter (growth environment)
- 🌳 Tree (hierarchy, structure)
- 💾 Hard drive (storage)
- 🏰 Castle (security/stability)

## Expression Prompts (Reference Guide)
When generating Terra, append these specific prompts to maintain the expression constraints:

1. **😆 Excited:** "Terra with wide, sparkling eyes and a huge open-mouthed smile, tiny limbs jumping slightly."
2. **😎 Confident:** "Terra with a cool smirk, sunglasses-style eye shape, standing tall and sturdy."
3. **😕 Confused:** "Terra with one raised eyebrow, squiggly mouth, tiny limbs scratching its head."
4. **😤 Frustrated:** "Terra with furrowed brows, a tight line for a mouth, tiny steam clouds from its head."
5. **💡 Eureka:** "Terra with bright wide eyes, a small smile, and a glowing lightbulb appearing above the teal moss."
6. **😊 Proud:** "Terra with a gentle, satisfied smile, eyes closed in a happy arc, standing with hands on hips."
7. **😴 Sleepy:** "Terra with half-closed heavy eyes, a small drool, and a soft Zzz floating above the moss."
8. **😨 Scared:** "Terra with wide, trembling eyes, mouth in a small 'o' shape, tiny limbs shaking."
9. **😏 Mischievous:** "Terra with a slanted smirk, half-lidded eyes looking sideways."
10. **😢 Crying:** "Terra with thick cartoon tears welling up, a sad wobbly mouth, looking down."
11. **😠 Angry:** "Terra with sharp slanted brows, a frowning mouth, looking very rigid and dense."
12. **🥰 Loving:** "Terra with soft, warm eyes, blushing cheeks, and small hearts floating around."
