# Spark (Lightning)

## 1. Visuals
- **Form/Shape**: Jagged lines, glowing energy, dynamic poses. 
- **Color Palette**: Bright yellow and electric blue.
- **Key Features**: Always floating or in motion, crackling energy.

## 2. Personality & Traits
- **Vibe**: Fast, energetic, impatient, bright, chaotic.
- **Speed/Pacing**: Lightning fast, erratic.

## 3. Metaphors & Ideal Use Cases
- **Represents**: Speed, instant execution, signals.
- **Technical Concepts**: APIs, real-time events, hotfixes, caching, low-latency networks.
- **Role in Illustration**: Moving quickly between other elements, bringing things to life, delivering messages.

## 4. Specific Expressions
- **Excited**: Vibrates rapidly, glows intensely bright.
- **Frustrated**: Sharp, erratic jagged angles; red sparks.
- **Confused**: Drooping, duller yellow, wavy instead of sharp.
