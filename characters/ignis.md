# Ignis (Fire)

## 1. Visuals
- **Form/Shape**: Flickering, intense, wispy or sharp flames. 
- **Color Palette**: Strictly black and white minimalist line art. NO colors.
- **Key Features**: Simple continuous black outlines, solid flat white fills, eyes are simple solid black dots, mouth is a simple thin black curved single line.

## 2. Personality & Traits
- **Vibe**: Passionate, intense, destructive yet transformative.
- **Speed/Pacing**: Aggressive, expanding.

## 3. Metaphors & Ideal Use Cases
- **Represents**: Destruction, execution, high energy.
- **Technical Concepts**: Deployment (going live), deleting/cleaning data, high CPU usage, burnout, "putting out fires" (debugging).
- **Role in Illustration**: Action-oriented, often showing high-energy states or removing obstacles.

## 4. Specific Expressions
- **Excited**: Flaring up tall, bright white-hot core.
- **Frustrated**: Dark red, producing heavy black smoke.
- **Confused**: Flickering, shrinking down to a small ember.

## 5. Universal Prompt Formulation
> A minimalist simple-line flat vector illustration on a pure white background of Ignis the Flame (a flickering, intense, wispy flame shape). Centered composition, ample negative space. Simple continuous solid black outlines, pure flat white fills. NO shading, NO gradients, NO 3D effects, NO colors. Eyes are simple solid black dots. Mouth is a simple thin black curved single line. Limbs are simple thin U-shaped stick lines.
