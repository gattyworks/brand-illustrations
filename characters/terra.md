# Terra (Rock)

## 1. Visuals
- **Form/Shape**: Sturdy, blocky, solid forms. 
- **Color Palette**: Earthy tones (browns, grays, moss green).
- **Key Features**: Heavy, textured surface. 

## 2. Personality & Traits
- **Vibe**: Grounded, reliable, unshakeable, stubborn.
- **Speed/Pacing**: Slow and steady, immovable.

## 3. Metaphors & Ideal Use Cases
- **Represents**: Foundation, stability, storage.
- **Technical Concepts**: Databases, immutable state, legacy code, solid architecture principles, hard drives.
- **Role in Illustration**: The anchor of an illustration, providing support or blocking something.

## 4. Specific Expressions
- **Excited**: Trembles slightly, moss/crystals might glow.
- **Frustrated**: Cracks appear, small rocks fall off.
- **Confused**: Looks like a blank slate, rigid.
