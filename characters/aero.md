# Aero (Wind)

## 1. Visuals
- **Form/Shape**: Light, breezy, cloud-like or swirling forms. 
- **Color Palette**: Whites, pale grays, and mint green.
- **Key Features**: Barely there, translucent, swirling lines.

## 2. Personality & Traits
- **Vibe**: Lighthearted, everywhere at once, unseen but felt.
- **Speed/Pacing**: Drifting, floating.

## 3. Metaphors & Ideal Use Cases
- **Represents**: The abstract, the invisible network.
- **Technical Concepts**: The Cloud, networks, Wi-Fi, serverless architecture, wireless connections.
- **Role in Illustration**: Overseeing things from above, carrying messages through the air.

## 4. Specific Expressions
- **Excited**: Breezy, blowing wind happily, expanding outward.
- **Frustrated**: Turns dark gray, small lightning bolts flash inside.
- **Confused**: Dissipating, becoming a thin hazy fog.
